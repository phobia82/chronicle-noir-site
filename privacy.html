Privacy Policy

This policy describes how the Chronicle Noir publishing app accesses, uses and stores Google and YouTube account data. It applies only to that internal tool.

Who uses the app

The Chronicle Noir app is used only by the channel owner. It is a personal, internal publishing tool. It is not offered to the public, and it does not collect data from other users, viewers or visitors to this website.

What the app accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, the app requests access to YouTube Data API v3 scopes needed to publish and maintain videos on the owner’s own Chronicle Noir channel (channel ID UCFtGP_eLWJ3ea-s7LvQxYEA). Those scopes are used only to:

  • upload videos to that channel;
  • manage those videos, including titles, descriptions, privacy settings and other metadata;
  • upload and manage thumbnails; and
  • upload and manage captions.

The app does not access YouTube data belonging to other people. It does not read, collect or store data from channel viewers, commenters or any other Google users.

How that data is used and stored

YouTube data is used solely to operate the owner’s publishing workflow for the Chronicle Noir channel. OAuth tokens are stored encrypted at rest on a private machine under the owner’s control. Tokens are not shared, sold, transferred or used for advertising, analytics products or any purpose unrelated to uploading and managing the owner’s own videos, thumbnails and captions.

Data we do not collect

This website does not run a user account system, and the app does not collect personal information from members of the public. No data from other users is collected, sold or shared.

Google and YouTube policy compliance

The app’s use of Google user data complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of the YouTube API is also subject to the YouTube API Services Terms of Service. Google’s own practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy.

How to revoke access

The owner can revoke the app’s access to their Google Account at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. After access is revoked, the stored tokens are no longer valid and should be deleted from the private machine.
