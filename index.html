Chronicle Noir

Chronicle Noir is a YouTube channel that publishes cinematic, faceless history documentaries. It is operated by its owner as an independent channel.

These pages exist so visitors — and Google’s OAuth consent screen — can see what the channel is, how the owner’s internal publishing tool works, and how YouTube account data is handled.

The Chronicle Noir app is a private publishing tool used only by the channel owner. It is not a public product, and it is not offered to other users. The owner uses it to upload and manage videos, thumbnails and captions on the Chronicle Noir YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API.
